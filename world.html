World

Indira Gandhi

World Population: 3.4 billion

Nobel Prizes:

  • Literature: Shmuel Yosef Agnon and Nelly Sachs
  • Chemistry: Robert S. Mulliken
  • Physics: Alfred Kastler
Science:
  • First Space Docking: On March 16, 1966, Gemini 8, piloted by Neil Armstrong and David Scott, achieved the first successful docking of two spacecraft in orbit.
  • Lunar Exploration: The US achieved its first soft lunar landing with Surveyor 1 on June 2, and the first lunar orbit by Lunar Orbiter 1 on August 10.
Other:
  • HP 2116A: Hewlett-Packard introduced this 16-bit minicomputer, aimed at instrument control and data processing.
  • Robert Taylor joined ARPA (Advanced Research Projects Agency) and brought Larry Roberts to develop ARPANET (Advanced Research Projects Agency Network).
  • First Turing Award
